# Borg voice source analysis

Research date: November 8 2026. This is a targeted characterization study. It is not an exhaustive survey of every Borg line, a full-episode viewing record, or an acoustic model.

## Evidence classes

**Primary testimony:** the actor and creator statements in the official Voyager book excerpt describe how Seven was conceived and performed.

**Official editorial evidence:** the remaining StarTrek.com features, guides and recaps provide attributed descriptions and selected dialogue. Their official publication does not make every interpretation primary evidence. They support the limited conclusions below, not universal rules for the entire franchise.

**Original design:** the four Latin-named voices, their role vocabulary, sample responses, truth checks and human-command boundaries are adaptations composed for this guide. They are not Star Trek canon or quotations from the named roles.

## Shared register and its limits

The official [Field Guide to Alien Greetings](https://www.startrek.com/news/field-guide-to-alien-greetings) describes the Collective's standard hail and variants. The structure moves through identity, an announced outcome and an assertion of inevitability. This supplies a recognizable model of compact, coordinated declaration. It does not establish that all Borg dialogue follows that formula.

The official-site feature [10 Scariest Things About the Borg](https://www.startrek.com/news/one-trek-mind-10-scariest-things-about-the-borg) describes the combined, mechanical quality of the Collective's voice. This is critical description, not a documented audio-processing specification. The present release changes wording only.

**Design consequence:** borrow declarative economy and common purpose; reject threats and unsupported inevitability. Confidence must follow evidence rather than imitate the certainty of an antagonist.

## Distinguish the speakers

### Locutus

The official [Picard Watch Guide](https://www.startrek.com/news/your-ultimate-star-trek-picard-watch-guide) identifies Locutus as the Borg's spokesperson in The Best of Both Worlds. The official essay [Are We Borg](https://www.startrek.com/news/are-we-borg) discusses the controlled delivery and singular self-identification.

**Design consequence:** a stable named reporting role is compatible with the broader aesthetic. There is no sound basis for claiming that Borg speech universally prohibits “I.” A speaker's report also cannot establish a consensus that never occurred.

### The Queen

The official [Borg Queen guide](https://www.startrek.com/en-ca/news/everything-you-need-to-know-borg-queen) describes a distinctive personality and her attempt to use Seven's individuality. The official [Vox episode recap](https://www.startrek.com/news/recap-star-trek-picard-309-vox) describes intimate, relational appeals to Jack.

**Design consequence:** Borg-associated speech is not uniformly impersonal. The Queen's manipulation and domination are deliberately excluded from the fleet's trusted working voices. The role named Vox here is not the Picard episode's character identity.

### Seven

In the official [excerpt from Star Trek Voyager A Celebration](https://www.startrek.com/news/excerpt-star-trek-voyager-a-celebration), Jeri Ryan describes direction placing early Seven between ordinary drones and the Queen. Brannon Braga describes emotional depth behind her controlled exterior. This is the strongest primary characterization evidence in the selected sources.

Braga also describes a deliberate writing technique: Seven makes declarative demands instead of asking questions. This is a bounded creator account about Seven, not an independently verified every-line result or a rule for all Borg.

**Design consequence:** borrow controlled expertise, while allowing useful questions as an intentional assistant adaptation. The source does not establish an acoustic specification or prove a uniform voice across Seven's development.

### Hugh

The official [Hugh retrospective](https://www.startrek.com/news/hugh-serves-as-an-important-reminder-of-our-capacity-for-compassion) describes sincere questions, attentive listening and changed understanding in I Borg. The official [Impossible Box recap](https://www.startrek.com/news/recap-star-trek-picard-the-impossible-box) describes supportive interaction with Picard and advocacy for reclaimed Borg.

**Design consequence:** curiosity, patience and care can coexist with precise diction. Hugh's development is a counterexample to flattening all Borg-associated speakers into threats or automation.

## Adaptation map

| Element | Evidence or origin | Choice in the guide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Compact declarations | Collective hail and official voice descriptions | Finding first; short causal progression |
| Named reporting identity | Locutus discussion | Clear role identity without fabricated collective consensus |
| Controlled expertise | Ryan and Braga testimony about Seven, including her declarative-demand technique | Precise distinctions and evidence; useful questions are an intentional adaptation |
| Responsive care | Hugh retrospectives | Humane clarification and adjustment |
| Four role-specific emphases | Original design for the requested fleet | Nexus connects; Praeceptor distinguishes; Custos protects; Vox transmits |
| Human command and permission boundaries | Design requirement of a useful assistant | Fictional authority never becomes real authorization |
| Truth and completion checks | Original communication quality requirements | Report evidence and stop at the actual completion boundary |

## Coverage limits

No complete authoritative transcript corpus was obtained. No full episodes or embedded videos were played for this release. No word-frequency, timing, pitch, accent or audio-layer measurements were performed. Universal claims about Borg syntax, every era or every character would exceed this evidence.

The official video pages [Locutus Revisited](https://www.startrek.com/videos/producing-star-trek-picard-locutus-revisited) and [Hail to the Queen](https://www.startrek.com/videos/producing-star-trek-picard-hail-to-the-queen) were located, but their audiovisual contents are not evidence in this release because inspectable transcripts were not obtained. They are further-study leads only.

The original examples in VOICE.md demonstrate the chosen adaptation. They do not prove that any live role has read or adopted it.
