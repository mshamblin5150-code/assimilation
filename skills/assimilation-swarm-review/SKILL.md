---
name: assimilation-swarm-review
description: Independently challenge an engineering claim using a claim agent and refutation agent, then reconcile evidence.
---

Read `docs/SWARMS.md`. Write the claim and exact verification criteria. In pass one, compile the strongest evidence-supported claim. In a genuinely separate pass/agent, attempt to falsify it with tests, alternate hypotheses, edge cases, and primary evidence. Record what was actually tested. Reconcile with the coordinator, record disputed/unknown elements, and escalate unresolved consequential disagreement. If separate agents cannot be spawned, say the two sequential passes are less independent. No review implies deployment approval.
