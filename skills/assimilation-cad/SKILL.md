---
name: assimilation-cad
description: Translate ordinary-language physical design ideas into parametric CAD with explicit dimensions and print verification.
---

Read `docs/THREE-D-PRINTING.md` and `AGENTS.md`. Clarify dimensions, mating surfaces, loads, material, printer constraints and tolerance. When necessary write a short design ADR. Use an installed CAD geometry tool to create/edit a parametric source. If an exporter actually runs, provide real STL/3MF and verify geometry. Otherwise provide source with execution instructions and mark binary output unavailable. Independently challenge fit/strength claims; require real-world physical prototype verification. Do not assert a part is load-bearing safe or print-ready solely on visual plausibility.
